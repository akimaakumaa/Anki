**[haven't even begun to think]** = Used to emphasize that a task is so large or you are so far behind that you haven't started the very first mental step.
Verb forms: begin /bɪˈgɪn/ — began /bɪˈgæn/ — begun /bɪˈgʌn/
Example 1: I haven't even begun to think about what I'm wearing to the wedding.
Example 2: We haven't even begun to think of a solution to that bug.
Watch out: Because it follows "have/haven't", you must use the V3 participle "begun", not the V2 "began".
