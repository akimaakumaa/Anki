Forms: arise /əˈraɪz/ — arose /əˈrəʊz/ — arisen /əˈrɪzən/
**arose** = Used in professional or serious spoken English to say that a situation, problem, or opportunity unexpectedly appeared or started to exist.

Example 1: I was going to call you earlier, but a complication arose with the client.
Example 2: A sudden conflict arose between the two departments over the budget.
