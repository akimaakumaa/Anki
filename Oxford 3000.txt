FRONT:

BACK:
verb+noun
load the bar = to put weight plates onto the barbell
Example 1: He loaded the bar with four plates for his heavy bench press set.
Example 2: Always load the bar carefully so it doesn't tip over.
Synonyms: prepare the barbell, add plates to the bar
